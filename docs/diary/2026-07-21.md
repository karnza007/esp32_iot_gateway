## Decisions made today

**Sample rate does not need to be a round number.** `fs = 24 MHz / (64 × N)` is an
integer divide, so every `N` gives an exact, jitter-free rate. 31.25 kHz is precisely as
accurate as 15 kHz; only the FFT axis label cares. The sweep will therefore use whatever
`N` values are convenient: 25, 16, 12, 10, 8.

**Maximum is `N = 8` → 46.875 kHz.** Bounded by the INMP441's 3.2 MHz SCK limit, not by
anything on our side. Two channels at that rate is 187.5 kB/s = **94 % of the 2 Mbaud
UART**, which is the intended saturation point.

**"Accuracy" is the wrong metric.** A digital link does not lose accuracy — bytes arrive
correct or the frame is corrupt. Split into **integrity** (checksum, framing errors) and
**fidelity** (SNR, THD, SINAD → ENOB). Loss is measured separately as **frame drop rate**.
Written up in `04-roadmap.md`.

**One FIFO is correct; ping-pong is not needed here.** A FIFO's independent read and
write pointers already give concurrent producer/consumer operation — that *is* continuous
flow. Ping-pong (double) buffering solves block-at-a-time handoff, which is a DMA
problem. It will genuinely be needed on the ESP32 side in the SPI milestone, and that is
a nice point for the report: the buffering strategy follows from the transport.
